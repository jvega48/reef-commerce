# Customer User Guide

How shoppers use the AquaVida365 storefront.

## Accounts

- **Register** (`/register`): email + password. An account earns Reef Points
  on every order and keeps order history.
- **Login** (`/login`): email + password. Repeated failed attempts are
  rate-limited.
- **Guest checkout** is supported — only an email address is required — but
  guests don't earn Reef Points.

## Shopping

- **Shop** (`/shop`): browse the full catalog with category and livestock-type
  filters, text search, and pagination. WYSIWYG corals are one-of-a-kind:
  the photo is the exact specimen you receive, and once someone buys it,
  it's gone.
- **Product pages**: photos, price, care profile (lighting, flow, placement,
  care level, tank size), availability.
- **Cart** (`/cart`): adjust quantities or remove items; stock is re-checked
  at checkout so you can't buy something that just sold.

## Checkout (`/checkout`)

1. Enter email (or sign in to earn points).
2. Choose delivery: **Overnight shipping** ($60, $40 within CA, free over
   $350 — live values shown at checkout) or **Local Pickup** (free).
3. Enter the shipping address (not needed for pickup).
4. Pay via Stripe — cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay. (Until payment keys are
   configured the store runs in a labeled test mode and no payment is taken.)
5. Confirmation page shows the order number.

Live orders ship Tuesday or Wednesday, overnight, to the lower 48 states —
full details on the [Shipping Policy](/shipping) page.

## After the order

- **Order history**: `/account` lists your orders and their status.
- **Delivery day**: someone should be present to receive the box. Acclimate
  livestock promptly.
- **Problem with an arrival?** The [Live Arrival Guarantee](/guarantee) page
  explains exactly what to do — the critical parts: video the unopened bag
  and the box **within 2 hours** of delivery for DOA claims, and report any
  concern **within 3 days**.
- **Returns**: no returns on livestock; cancellations receive store credit
  or a refund minus the restocking fee (see the guarantee page).

## Reef Points

Account holders earn **1 point per dollar** on every completed order. Your
balance shows in `/account`. (Redemption at checkout arrives in an upcoming
release.)
